THE KETTURAM LEGACY & STORY

Echoes of the Ancient Waterways

Born from four decades of master boatwright traditions in Kuttanad, Ketturam preserves the sacred, nail-free art of kettuvallam crafting while offering discerning voyagers private, eco-conscious sanctuaries across Kerala’s tranquil palm lagoons.

Founding Heritage (1978)

From Historic Spice Barges to Luxury Floating Sanctuaries

Before asphalt highways intersected the palm labyrinths of Alleppey and Kottayam, massive wooden vessels known as kettuvallam ruled the Vembanad waters.

Carrying mounds of freshly harvested Tellicherry black pepper, cardamom, dried copra, and aromatic red matta rice from the fertile Kuttanad plains to Cochin port, these majestic barges were the lifeblood of Malabar trade. Each vessel could haul up to thirty tons of cargo, steered solely by a long bamboo pole and the navigator’s intimate knowledge of the lunar tides.

“When you sew timber with coconut fiber, the boat breathes with the water. An iron nail rusts, weakens, and fights the swell; coir yields, bends, and survives for a century.”

Eappen Varghese, Master Boatwright & Founder (1938–2014)

In the late 1980s, as motor trucks replaced traditional river transport, Eappen Varghese refused to allow this ancestral maritime architecture to vanish. Instead, he pioneered the very first bespoke residential kettuvallams—preserving the hand-stitched hull intact while sculpting private teakwood staterooms, breezy viewing verandas, and a private galley for authentic coastal feasts.

Ancestral Naval Architecture

The Four Sacred Steps of Kettuvallam Crafting

Literally translating to “boat with knots” (Kettu = to tie, Vallam = boat), our vessels are built without a single metallic fastener, using techniques passed down orally across generations.

01

Wild Anjili Wood

Harvested sustainably from fallen wild Jackwood (Artocarpus hirsutus). Thick planks are cured submerged in brackish water mud for 6 months to repel borer insects and rot.

Submerged 180 Days
02

Hand-Twisted Coir

Carpenters carve precise grooved seams and sew the planks together using hand-spun coconut coir twine. Between the joints, woven coconut fiber cushions prevent leaks.

Zero Iron Nails
03

Halmadder & Resin Pitch

The sewn seams are coated with panampasi—a boiled mixture of cashew shell resin, melted bone pitch, and indigenous fish oil that creates a supple, flexible waterproof bond.

100% Water-Tolerant
04

Bamboo & Thatch Canopy

The vaulted superstructure (Valavara) is bent by hand using resilient bamboo poles and thatched with dried palm fronds, providing natural microclimate cooling.

Our Hospitality Ethos

We believe true luxury is quiet, unhurried, and deeply rooted in the natural rhythms of the backwaters.

Every booking is an exclusive private charter. You never share your deck, dining area, or crew with strangers. From sunrise mist to starlit night moorings, the boat is your private estate.

Lagoon-to-Galley Dining

Fresh Pearl Spot (Karimeen) straight from lagoon cast-nets, aromatic duck roast braised in stone-ground spices, and hot appams made fresh on demand. Pure vegetarian and Jain banquets prepared with utmost care.

Environmental Stewardship

The Green Canal Pledge

The backwaters are not our business backdrop—they are our ancestral home. We safeguard their delicate ecology with unwavering commitment.

Vembanad Lake is an internationally recognized Ramsar wetland. To ensure our cruises nourish rather than burden this ecosystem, Ketturam adheres to four strict ecological standards across every charter.

From zero single-use plastics and solar silent cruising to DRDO bio-digestion, our fleet preserves the natural sanctity of God's Own Country.

Learn About Carbon-Neutral Charters

100% Bio-Digester Systems

DRDO-approved microbial digestion units digest all onboard waste into harmless water and biogas, preventing a single drop from entering the lake.

Silent Sanctuary Corridors

When passing the Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary and Pathiramanal Island, our boats switch to quiet electric auxiliary propulsion to safeguard nesting avifauna.

Zero Single-Use Plastics

Drinking water is served in sterilized copper carafes and reusable glass bottles. Meals and trail snacks are wrapped in fresh banana leaves.

Mangrove Reforestation Fund

For every two nights chartered aboard our fleet, Ketturam funds the planting and 3-year care of one native red mangrove sapling in the Kuttanad delta.
