How to Reach Us

  • Cochin International Airport (COK): ~85 km (2 hours via NH 66 highway). Private taxi pickup available.
  • Alleppey Railway Station (ALLP): ~4.5 km (12 minutes). Direct auto-rickshaws and cabs available.
  • Secured Jetty Parking: Free 24-hour guarded car parking available for our guests directly at the jetty.

Boarding & Harbour Privileges

  • Traditional Welcome Reception: Chilled fresh tender coconut drink, fragrant jasmine towels, and luggage porterage upon arrival.
  • Dedicated 3-Member Crew: Certified Master Captain, marine engine driver, and personal private chef on every voyage.
  • Tailored Cruise Timings: Flexible meal times and custom scenic stopovers arranged directly with your onboard captain.
